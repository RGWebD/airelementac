About & FAQ

Local. Licensed. Family-owned.

A Mandarin-based heating and air conditioning company keeping homes and businesses comfortable across four Northeast Florida counties.

The company

“Great family owned local business.”

That’s how customer Lieth Quffa put it — and the company’s own reply says the same: “our family-owned business.” Air Element AC and Heating LLC works out of a Mandarin office on Hood Road and handles residential and commercial heating and cooling: repairs, replacements, duct systems, mini-splits and rooftop equipment.

In their own words: “Don’t wait until the sun starts blazing or the weather drops to freezing temperatures to call for HVAC assistance. Let us keep your home or business at the temperatures you find most comfortable.”

Reviewers keep coming back to the same things: technicians who show up fast, explain the options, don’t upsell and leave the place clean.
